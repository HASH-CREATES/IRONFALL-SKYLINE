## 4. Rejected / not used

- Kenney City Kit (CC0) — evaluated; procedural authoring gave tighter flight-corridor
  control and a smaller payload. Not downloaded. **Nothing from Kenney ships.**
- Sketchfab / TurboSquid "Iron Man" models — **rejected on license grounds**
  (fan-art and Marvel IP; non-commercial or unclear terms). Marvel-owned villain
  models (e.g. Iron Monger, Whiplash drones) — **rejected on license grounds**;
  the Null Syndicate uses CC0 robot designs instead (Section 1b).
- Poly by Google models — CC-BY 3.0 candidates evaluated (helicopters) but the
  kazuma CC0 helicopter was preferred to minimize attribution burden.
- Any movie SFX/music — rejected (copyright).
